How It Works

Simple steps to get your issue resolved.

1

Register / Login

Create an account using your mobile number or citizen ID to verify your identity.

2

Submit Details

Select the department, describe your issue, and upload supporting documents.

3

Get Acknowledged

Receive an immediate SMS/Email with a unique reference number for tracking.

4

Resolution

The concerned officer reviews the case and updates the status. You will be notified.
